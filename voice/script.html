Recording script for Sanau

Enkaji Play speaks to children and mothers in the Maasai Mara. These lines will be played in your voice instead of a computer voice, so children hear a real Kenyan accent.

How to record (about 10 minutes)
  1. Find a quiet place, with no radio, animals or wind. Indoors is best.
  2. Use the phone's voice recorder (or a WhatsApp voice note). Hold the phone a hand's width from your mouth.
  3. Read each line once, in a warm, friendly voice, as if talking to a child. Then stay quiet for 2 seconds before the next line.
  4. Do not say the numbers. If you make a mistake, pause, then say the line again.
  5. You can record each part as a separate file. Send the files back.
