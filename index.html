Enkaji means "home" in Maa. Six Bricks is a LEGO Foundation tool; learn more from Care for Education. Built for facilitators and Mothers Who Teach in the Maasai Mara.

📲 Put Enkaji Play on your phone

It installs like an app, with its own icon, and works without signal after the first time.

No good signal? Download the Android APK once, then share it phone to phone by Bluetooth or Xender. It works with no internet at all.
